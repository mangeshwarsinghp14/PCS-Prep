**Q1.** In a meeting Rajita always begins her sentences with 'I may be wrong, but…' or, 'Excuse my impertinence, but…' What style of communication is Rajita following ?

(a) Assertive  
(b) Passive aggressive or Manipulative  
(c) Passive  
(d) None of the above

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Passive** — Habitually apologising before speaking ('I may be wrong, but…') puts your own view below others'. That is a passive style of communication.

**Q5.** Read these six conditional claims :  
1. If it's an apple, then it's a fruit.  
2. If it's a fruit, then it's an apple.  
3. It's an apple if it's a fruit.  
4. It's a fruit if it's an apple.  
5. It's not a fruit if it's not an apple.  
6. It's not an apple if it's not a fruit.  
Out of these six conditional claims which three claims have the answer as Yes ?

(a) 2, 3, 5  
(b) 2, 3, 4  
(c) 3, 4, 5  
(d) 1, 4, 6

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) 1, 4, 6** — 'Apple → fruit' is true. Claim 1 says this, claim 4 restates it, and claim 6 is its contrapositive (not fruit → not apple). Claims 2, 3 and 5 all say 'fruit → apple', which is false.
