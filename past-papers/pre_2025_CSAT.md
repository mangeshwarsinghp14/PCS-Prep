**Q10.** If a^3 − b^3 = 91 and a − b = 1, what is the value of (ab) ?

(a) 27  
(b) 6  
(c) 9  
(d) 30

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) 30** — a^3 − b^3 = (a − b)(a^2 + ab + b^2) gives a^2 + ab + b^2 = 91; (a − b)^2 = 1 gives a^2 + b^2 − 2ab = 1; subtracting, 3ab = 90, ab = 30.
