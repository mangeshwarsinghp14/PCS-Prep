**Q3.** Write a letter to the editor of a national newspaper about street begging and suggest the ways to tackle it. OR You are the Student Union in-charge of your college. Write a letter to the Dean of your institution regarding the deteriorating mental health of students due to exam pressure and competitiveness. Suggest ways to create a healthier environment for students. *(10 marks)*

<details><summary>Model answer points</summary>

*Why it matters:* A formal letter (editor/official) is set every year.

*Intro:* Use formal letter format: sender's address, date, receiver, subject, salutation, body, closing, name.

*Points:*
- Option A (editor): subject line; describe the problem at traffic lights/religious places; causes — poverty, organised begging rackets, child exploitation; effects — safety, image of city.
- Suggestions: rescue and rehabilitation through social welfare department, shelter homes, skill training, action against rackets, awareness not to give cash, NGO partnerships; Supreme Court/High Court views on decriminalising begging can be referred to generally.
- Option B (Dean): state concern with examples (stress, anxiety, isolation); causes — exam pressure, competition, career uncertainty.
- Suggestions: counselling cell, mentoring, wellness week, sports/yoga, flexible assessment, peer support groups, helpline, faculty sensitisation.
- Close politely requesting action; keep to about 200 words.

**Q4.** Write an essay on any one of the following in about 300 words: (a) Work from home during Pandemic: pros & cons (b) Unity is strength but uniformity could be disastrous. (c) Judicial delays kill the spirit of justice. *(10 marks)*

<details><summary>Model answer points</summary>

*Why it matters:* Short essay is fixed in every English paper.

*Intro:* Plan: introduction, 3 body paragraphs, conclusion; about 300 words.

*Points:*
- (a) Pros: flexibility, saved commute, productivity for some, inclusion of disabled/women; cons: isolation, blurred work hours, digital divide, weak team culture; conclusion — hybrid model.
- (b) Unity in diversity; strength from cooperation; uniformity suppresses creativity, minority cultures, federalism; India's pluralism as example; conclusion — unity with respect for difference.
- (c) 'Justice delayed is justice denied'; crores of pending cases; undertrials; causes — vacancies, adjournments, poor infrastructure; reforms — e-courts, fast-track courts, ADR/Lok Adalats, more judges.

**Q13.** Rewrite the given paragraph using appropriate punctuation marks. (A short unpunctuated paragraph of dialogue between the writer and a colleague named Atima about an experiment.) *(5 marks)*

<details><summary>Model answer points</summary>

*Why it matters:* Punctuation is a regular 5-mark item.

*Intro:* Key points to apply:

*Points:*
- Put reported speech in quotation marks with a comma before it: Atima, who was not involved in this experiment, said, "Based on your own …, I and others find it hard to believe …."
- Set off the non-defining clause 'who was not involved in this experiment' with commas.
- Question inside speech ends with a question mark: "… can I believe you either?"
- Use a comma after the introductory 'Well' and an exclamation mark for 'What a relief!'
- Capitalise the first word of each quotation and start a new paragraph for each new speaker.
