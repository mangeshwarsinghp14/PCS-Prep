## Exam pattern

| Prelims paper | Questions and marks | Time | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper I — General Studies | 100 Qs × 2 = 200 | 2 hours | Decides merit for Mains |
| Paper II — CSAT | 80 Qs × 2.5 = 200 | 2 hours | Qualifying: 40% (80 marks) |

- No negative marking in either paper.
- Paper is bilingual — English and Punjabi.
- 12–13 times the vacancies (category-wise) go through to Mains.
- Prelims marks are not added to the final merit.

| Mains paper | Marks |
|---|---|
| Punjabi (Gurmukhi), 10+2 level | 100 |
| English, 10+2 level | 100 |
| Essay — three short essays | 150 |
| GS I — History, Geography, Society | 250 |
| GS II — Constitution, Polity, Governance, IR | 250 |
| GS III — Economy, Statistics, Security | 250 |
| GS IV — Sci & Tech, Environment, Problem solving | 250 |
| Interview | 150 |

- All papers descriptive, 3 hours each; answer in English or Punjabi (except language papers).
- No optional subject.
- To reach interview: 45% aggregate (40% SC/ST) and at least 25% in every paper.
- Final merit = Mains written + Interview = 1,500.
- Official note: special emphasis on history, culture, economy and geography of Punjab in GS.
